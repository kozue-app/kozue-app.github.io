Feed reader for Windows

梢Your feeds, no algorithm, no rush.

Kozue brings the websites, blogs, podcasts and channels you follow into one place. It works on your PC alone, with no accounts or servers, or with the feed service you already use.

Microsoft Store free Download the installer

No server, or your own

Choose when you first open Kozue. You can have several accounts and switch between them.

On this PC

Paste a website address and Kozue finds its feed, downloads it and keeps it on your PC. No sign-up needed.

<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml" href="/feed.xml">

With your service

Read the same as on your phone or other computers: Kozue syncs with the feed service you already use.

  • Miniflux
  • FreshRSS
  • Inoreader
  • Feedbin
  • Nextcloud News
  • NewsBlur
  • Tiny Tiny RSS
  • The Old Reader
  • Google Reader API
  • Fever API

Made for calm reading

Full text

Fetches the whole article from its website, without menus, sidebars or ads.

Offline

The latest articles and your favorites stay on your PC. On the train or with the network down, you keep reading.

Timeline

Kozue remembers where you were in each view, like a bookmark.

Podcasts and video

A player that remembers your position and a floating window to keep watching while you read. Radio and YouTube and Twitch live streams too.

Keyboard first

j k to move, space to scroll, m to mark as read. And designed for Narrator and NVDA.

In your language

Spanish, English, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Hindi.

Light, dark and your way

Choose the font, size, column width and theme. Kozue can follow the Windows theme.

Organize with folders, favorites, tags and links to read later, and create filters by feed type or by words.

Kozue has no servers

  • It collects no usage data, has no ads or analytics, and sends nothing to its developer.
  • Your passwords are stored encrypted with Windows data protection.
  • It only connects to what you choose: your feeds, your articles’ websites and your service.
  • Full privacy policy
